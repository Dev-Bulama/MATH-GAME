## 🏎️ Deploy 3D Racing Game (5 Minutes!)

### Step 1: Get a Netlify Account
1. Go to **[netlify.com](https://www.netlify.com/)**
2. Click **"Sign up"**
3. Choose **"GitHub"** to sign up

### Step 2: Deploy This Repository
1. Click **"Add new site"** → **"Import an existing project"**
2. Click **"Deploy with GitHub"**
3. Select this repository: **`MATH-GAME`**
4. In settings, enter:
   - **Base directory:** `racing-game`
   - Leave everything else empty
5. Click **"Deploy site"**

### Step 3: Done! 🎉
- Wait 30 seconds
- Copy your game URL (looks like: `https://something.netlify.app`)
- Share with students!

**Your game is now live!** Works on phones, tablets, computers - everywhere!

## 📚 Deploy Streamlit Game (Alternative)

### Step 1: Get a Streamlit Account
1. Go to **[streamlit.io/cloud](https://streamlit.io/cloud)**
2. Click **"Sign up"** with GitHub

### Step 2: Deploy
1. Click **"New app"**
2. Select your **`MATH-GAME`** repository
3. Main file: **`app.py`**
4. Click **"Deploy!"**

### Step 3: Done! 🎉
- Wait 2-3 minutes
- Copy your game URL
- Share with students!
